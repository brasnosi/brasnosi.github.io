Privacy and personal data.

How the public website and email enquiries use information. Updated 13 November 2026.

01

Contact and scope

For questions about this website or your personal data, write to moje@brasno.si. The brasno.si contact location is 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia. This notice covers the public website and enquiries sent to that address. Please send only the contact and business information needed for your request.

02

Tools, cookies and browser storage

The calculator, search and enquiry preparation run in your browser. The site does not set tracking cookies or save form fields in browser storage. Preparing an enquiry does not send it to a database. Copying puts the text on your clipboard; opening the email link passes it to your email app. Prefilled values may remain in your browser history. No advertising analytics, social embeds or remote fonts are loaded.

03

When you send an email

Your name, work contact details, company and message are used to answer the enquiry and discuss a quotation. Processing business contact details relies on the legitimate interest in responding to requested business correspondence; steps toward a contract with you personally may rely on GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. The website makes no automated decisions about you.

04

Hosting and service providers

GitHub Pages handles website delivery and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Cloudflare provides DNS; network providers can process technical connection data. Email is handled by the sender's provider and the provider serving the recipient's mailbox. Providers may process data in other countries under their applicable arrangements and privacy notices. Social links contact external services only when you open them.

05

How long information is kept

The website has no enquiry database. Email correspondence is retained only while needed to handle the request, a resulting business relationship, applicable record-keeping duties or legal claims. Hosting and mail providers apply their own retention rules. Ask at moje@brasno.si about a particular message or deletion request; legal retention duties may limit deletion.

06

Your rights

Depending on the legal conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact moje@brasno.si to make a request. You may also complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, ip-rs.si) or another competent data-protection authority.
