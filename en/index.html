FAQ

Before the first order.

How do I request a flour quotation?+

Send the flour type, quantity, destination and supply frequency. The quotation confirms the product specification, availability and terms.

Can I test the flour first?+

Request a sample and tell us what you produce. Sample availability and arrangements are confirmed individually.

Do the type numbers mean the same thing in every country?+

No. National classifications are not automatically interchangeable. Compare the declared specification and test the flour in your process.

Where can you deliver?+

The business is based in Ljubljana. We assess the destination, volume and delivery requirements for each enquiry; a language version does not imply an active delivery network in that country.
